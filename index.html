Why Sacred
Calling Exists

Sacred Calling exists as a ministry to and for Christian leaders — to foster devotion to Christ and faithful service in His name.

In a time when many leaders carry deep burdens, Sacred Calling seeks to provide a place of renewal, clarity, encouragement, and strengthening. This ministry is centered on helping leaders remain close to Jesus while serving others with wisdom, mercy, and faithfulness.

We are guided by two simple but profound commands of Christ — Follow Me and Feed Them — and we believe these form the heartbeat of faithful Christian leadership.

What We Do

How We Serve

Sacred Calling comes alongside Christian leaders in four essential ways — meeting them where they are.

Spiritual
Direction

Helping leaders slow down, listen to God, and cultivate deeper attentiveness to the presence of Jesus. In a world of noise, we make space for the still small voice.

Mentorship &
Formation

Encouraging healthy growth through intentional relationships, guidance, and ministry support. Formation happens in the context of faithful community.

Biblical
Counseling

Providing biblically grounded care, encouragement, and support for leaders navigating the real pressures and challenges of faithful ministry life.

Gatherings &
Community

Hosting conferences, local meetings, and ministry environments that strengthen devotion, service, and fellowship among Christian leaders.

Personal Encouragement Session

Spiritual Direction
& Coaching

In a Personal Encouragement Session, Dr. Davenport meets with leaders, couples, and ministry teams to listen, encourage, and pray with them — offering Christ-centered spiritual direction and coaching for their life and calling.

Through guided conversations with Dr. Davenport, participants are invited to slow down, listen deeply, discern faithfully, and take practical next steps in life and ministry.

These calls create space for honest reflection, biblical encouragement, spiritual formation, leadership care, and practical ministry support.

What Guides Us

Our Commitments

01

The Presence
of Jesus

We believe true ministry begins — and is sustained — in communion with Christ. Everything flows from His presence.

02

A Culture
of Grace

We want every interaction to reflect the kindness and truth of Jesus — a culture where leaders find welcome, not judgment.

03

A Mission
of Mercy

We are called to serve others with compassion, humility, and love — mercy shaped by the heart of the Good Shepherd.

04

A Commitment to Righteousness

We pursue integrity, holiness, and faithfulness in every area of life and ministry — not as performance, but as devotion.
